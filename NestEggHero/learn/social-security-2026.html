Social Security in 2026: COLA and taxable maximum

5 min | Final SSA figures | Reviewed 2026-07-16 | Effective year 2026

What you will learn

  • What COLA means
  • What the OASDI wage base is
  • Why Medicare differs

Plain-language summary

The 2026 Social Security COLA is 2.8%. The 2026 OASDI taxable maximum is $184,500. SSA shows employee combined withholding of 7.65% and self-employed combined rate of 15.30%.

COLA follows prices

The cost-of-living adjustment changes benefits to help keep pace with inflation. It can be different every year.

The wage base

The OASDI component rate is 6.2% up to $184,500. Medicare rules differ and are not capped the same way.

Example

A worker earning more than $184,500 in 2026 pays the OASDI component only up to that base. Medicare withholding has separate rules.

Risks and exceptions

  • Personal benefits depend on earnings and claiming age.
  • Medicare rules are separate.
  • SSA figures change annually.

Key takeaways

  • The 2026 COLA is 2.8%.
  • The 2026 OASDI wage base is $184,500.
  • Self-employed people cover both halves.
