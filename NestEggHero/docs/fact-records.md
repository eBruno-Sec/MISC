# NestEggHero Fact Records

Last reviewed: 2026-07-16  
Next scheduled review: 2027-01-15

All changing financial facts used by the app live in `scripts/facts.js`. A fact record must include:

- Claim
- Value and display value
- Effective year
- Jurisdiction
- Authority status
- Source title and URL
- Retrieval date
- Reviewer
- Review date
- Next-review date
- Publication status

The app must not publish a changing financial fact from article prose unless it resolves through the registry. Calculator results are estimates only and must show assumptions beside outputs.
