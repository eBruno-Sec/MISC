DAY 12

The Talking Stick

Listening SkillsRespectPatienceSelf-ControlCommunication

🗣️ Chapter 1: Always Talking, Never Hearing

Jonah loved to talk. He talked during breakfast, he talked during class, and he even talked over his little brother when he was trying to share something.

One day at school, his teacher said, “Jonah, I know your voice is full of ideas, but we need to make space for others too.”

Jonah looked confused. “But I just get so excited!”

🌿 Chapter 2: The Circle of Voices

That afternoon, the class sat in a circle for a new activity. In the center was a stick, carved with little shapes.

“This,” said the teacher, “is our Talking Stick. When you hold it, you speak. When you don’t have it, you listen.”

Jonah fidgeted. Waiting was hard.

👂 Chapter 3: The Quiet Magic

Jonah watched as his friends shared stories, dreams, and feelings. He began to notice things he’d never heard before—like how his friend Maya felt nervous speaking in front of others, or how Eli loved painting.

“I didn’t know that,” Jonah whispered to himself.

🤲 Chapter 4: Jonah’s Turn

When the Talking Stick came to Jonah, he smiled. “Today, I listened. And I learned a lot.”

Everyone clapped softly.

Jonah realized something: his voice mattered, but so did everyone else’s. Listening wasn’t boring—it was powerful.

🏡 Chapter 5: Home Practice

That evening, Jonah brought the idea home. “Let’s try something,” he told his family. He used a spoon as a pretend Talking Stick at dinner.

One by one, everyone got to share while the others listened. Even his little brother got to finish a story.

Jonah grinned. “I’m learning to wait. And hear what people are really saying.”

🌙 Chapter 6: Jonah’s Listening Promise

Before bed, Jonah whispered to himself,

“I will use my ears like strong wings.
I will wait and hear with care.
My voice is special—but so are theirs.”

🌱 Reflect Together

  • What do you learn when you listen instead of talk?
  • How does it feel when someone lets you finish your thoughts?
  • Why is waiting your turn important during a conversation?
  • Can you try a “Talking Stick” moment with your family tomorrow?
