Day 12

The Talking Stick

Listening SkillsRespectPatienceSelf-ControlCommunication

💡 What I Learned

That evening, Jonah brought the idea home. “Let’s try something,” he told his family. He used a spoon as a pretend Talking Stick at dinner.

One by one, everyone got to share while the others listened. Even his little brother got to finish a story.

Jonah grinned. “I’m learning to wait. And hear what people are really saying.”

🌙 Chapter 6: Jonah’s Listening Promise

Before bed, Jonah whispered to himself,

“I will use my ears like strong wings.
I will wait and hear with care.
My voice is special—but so are theirs.”

🧠 Reflect & Inspire

  • What do you learn when you listen instead of talk?
  • How does it feel when someone lets you finish your thoughts?
  • Why is waiting your turn important during a conversation?
  • Can you try a “Talking Stick” moment with your family tomorrow?
