DAY 16

Eli and the Little Lion Voice

ConfidenceCourageSelf-BeliefTaking ActionInner Voice

🦁 Chapter 1: The Voice That Hid

Eli loved to think big but speak small. In class, he had good answers but stayed quiet. At karate, he knew the moves but stepped back during group demos.

“I want to be braver,” he told his dad. “But my voice… hides.”

Dad gave him a hug and said, “Then let’s help it grow. One small roar at a time.”

🐾 Chapter 2: The Little Lion

That night, Eli dreamed of a tiny lion in his chest.

“I’m your Brave Voice,” the lion said. “Every time you speak, try, or stand tall, I get stronger.”

“But what if I mess up?” Eli asked.

“Then I’ll still grow—because trying makes me roar.”

🎤 Chapter 3: A Brave Little Step

The next morning, Eli raised his hand once in class. His heart beat fast—but he did it!

“Good idea, Eli,” said his teacher. The lion inside gave a tiny growl of pride.

At practice, he offered to lead one move. It wasn’t perfect—but he did it loud and proud.

🔥 Chapter 4: The Roar Gets Louder

Each time Eli tried, the lion grew louder.

“You’re getting braver,” it said. “Mistakes are just steps. Confidence means showing up, even with shaky legs.”

One day, Eli said out loud, “I’m proud of me today.”

His little lion gave a mighty roar.

🏆 Chapter 5: Eli’s Big Moment

At the school show, Eli felt nervous. But he took a deep breath and imagined the lion inside him standing tall.

He played his song. Not perfect—but confident. The crowd clapped. Eli smiled, not because he was the best… but because he was brave.

🦁 Chapter 6: Eli’s Roar Promise

Before bed, Eli whispered,

“My voice is strong, my heart is wide.
I believe in me, from deep inside.
I try with courage every day.
My lion grows with what I say.”

🌱 Reflect Together

  • When do you feel your Brave Voice trying to speak?
  • What’s one thing you did today that made your lion grow stronger?
  • Can you remember a time you tried even if you felt nervous?
  • How can you help someone else feel confident too?
