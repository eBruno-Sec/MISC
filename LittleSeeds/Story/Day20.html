Day 20

Niko and the Falling Tower

ResilienceGrowth MindsetGritLearning from MistakesSelf-Compassion

💡 What I Learned

That night, Niko told his dad, “I built it again. It fell the first time, but I figured out why.”

His dad smiled. “Mistakes are the best teachers, Niko. You didn’t fail—you learned.”

“Next time,” Niko said, “I’m going to build it even stronger.”

🏗️ Chapter 5: The Builder’s Heart

From that day on, Niko was proud of every try—even the messy ones. He knew each attempt made him wiser, steadier, and stronger inside.

He became not just a builder of towers—but a builder of courage, too.

🧠 Chapter 6: Niko’s Builder Promise

Before bed, Niko whispered,

“If I fall, I’ll rise again.
Every mistake makes me a better man.
I will try, learn, and never quit—
My heart is strong, and I won’t sit.”

🪜 Reflect & Inspire

  • Have you ever felt upset when something didn’t work out?
  • What helped you calm down and try again?
  • What can you learn from making a mistake?
  • What’s something big you want to try, even if it takes a few tries?
