DAY 7

The Thankful Tree

GratitudeFamilyMindfulnessHappinessPerspective

🍂 Chapter 1: The Grumbly Morning

It was a chilly morning when Liam woke up with a frown. His blanket was warm, but he grumbled anyway.

“Why do I have to get up?” he sighed. “Why can’t I just play all day?”

He didn’t like his breakfast. He didn’t want to wear socks. And his little sister, Mia, had taken the blue crayon he liked.

“Ugh. Nothing goes my way,” Liam mumbled.

Mom noticed and sat down beside him. “Liam, when you focus on what’s missing, you miss what’s already good.”

🌳 Chapter 2: The Tree Behind the Fence

After school, Liam went outside and saw the old tree behind their fence. It had big branches, golden leaves, and the softest shade under it.

Grandpa was sitting there in his lawn chair, sipping tea and smiling at the wind.

“What are you smiling at, Grandpa?” Liam asked.

“The wind, the tree, the tea, and you.” Grandpa chuckled. “I call this our Thankful Tree. Every day, I come here and think of three things I’m grateful for.”

“Why?”

“Because being thankful turns a simple day into a great one.”

✋ Chapter 3: Three Things

Liam sat down next to Grandpa. He thought for a moment. “Umm... I guess I like the sun today. It’s not too hot.”

“That’s one,” Grandpa said, holding up a finger.

“And... my dog, Rusty. He’s funny.”

“That’s two.”

Liam thought harder. “And... I guess having you to talk to.”

Grandpa smiled and held up three fingers. “See? Not so hard. Your heart’s already smiling.”

📒 Chapter 4: A Jar of Thanks

That night, Mom gave Liam a small glass jar and a stack of paper.

“This is your Gratitude Jar. Each night, write down one thing you’re thankful for. Even a small thing.”

Liam rolled his eyes but gave it a try. He wrote, “Rusty made me laugh when he chased his tail.”

He dropped it into the jar and went to bed with a little smile.

📆 Chapter 5: A Week of Thanks

Every night, Liam added something new to the jar:

  • “Mia shared her cookie.”
  • “The sky looked like a painting.”
  • “I didn’t cry when I scraped my knee.”
  • “Dad read me an extra story.”
  • “I helped clean and Mom said thank you.”

The jar was filling up, and so was Liam’s heart.

He even noticed that Mia had started a little jar of her own.

🛏️ Chapter 6: The Thankful Promise

Before bedtime, Liam sat with Mom, both holding their jars.

“Did you know,” Mom said, “that when we are thankful, we see more of the good in life?”

Liam nodded. “It makes me feel happy, even when the day wasn’t perfect.”

He whispered his promise as he snuggled under the covers.

“I will notice the good things, even small ones.
I will say thank you and mean it.
I will grow my heart with gratitude.”

🌱 Reflect Together

  • What are three things you are thankful for today?
  • Did someone do something kind for you? How did it make you feel?
  • What is one thing in your room, house, or school that you’re glad to have?
  • Can you start your own Gratitude Jar? What would go in it first?
