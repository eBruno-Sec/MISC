Day 9

Jonah and the Broken Pen

Emotional StrengthCalming DownResilienceSelf-RegulationProblem Solving

💡 What I Learned

Jonah got an idea. He taped the pen together and made a label that read: “Battle Pen – Wounded but Brave.”

Then he got another pen from his drawer—one he had ignored before. “This one still works,” he said.

He gave Eli a high five. “Wanna draw with me?”

🌟 Chapter 5: Stronger Than a Pen

Jonah didn’t forget the pen breaking—but he learned something more important.

He could feel big emotions… and calm them down.

He wasn’t a cry-baby. He was growing braver, smarter, and stronger.

🛏️ Chapter 6: Jonah’s Quiet Power

That night, Jonah whispered the Calm-Down Code again:

“Stop. Breathe. Think. Try.
I don’t let anger or sadness win.
I am calm, clever, and in control.”

🌈 Reflect & Inspire

  • Can you remember the Calm-Down Code: Stop, Breathe, Think, Try?
  • What do you feel in your body when you're upset?
  • What helps you feel calm again?
  • How do you feel after calming down instead of staying upset?
