DAY 9

Jonah and the Broken Pen

Emotional StrengthCalming DownResilienceSelf-RegulationProblem Solving

🖊️ Chapter 1: The Favorite Pen

Jonah had a blue pen he loved more than any other. He used it for drawing, writing, and even signing pretend autographs.

It wasn’t just any pen—it was his “lucky pen.” He felt strong and smart when he held it.

One day, while trying to draw a racing car really fast, Jonah pressed too hard. *Snap!* The pen cracked in half.

“NOOO!” Jonah shouted. His heart sank. Tears filled his eyes, and he threw the broken pen on the floor.

🌧️ Chapter 2: Big Feelings

Jonah sat on the carpet, arms crossed, frowning. “This is the worst day ever,” he mumbled.

His little brother, Eli, tried to bring him a crayon. “Go away!” Jonah snapped.

Mom sat beside him. “It’s okay to feel sad. But you don’t have to stay stuck there.”

“But my pen is ruined,” Jonah sniffed.

“Let’s teach your brain a magic trick,” Mom whispered.

🧘 Chapter 3: The Calm-Down Code

Mom showed Jonah a quiet code: **Stop. Breathe. Think. Try.**

  • Stop moving and freeze like a statue.
  • Breathe in slowly, then out.
  • Think about what happened—and how to fix it.
  • Try something that makes it better.

Jonah tried. He sat still. He took a slow breath. Then another.

His shoulders relaxed. His heart beat softer. “I feel... okay,” he said quietly.

🔧 Chapter 4: The Backup Plan

Jonah got an idea. He taped the pen together and made a label that read: “Battle Pen – Wounded but Brave.”

Then he got another pen from his drawer—one he had ignored before. “This one still works,” he said.

He gave Eli a high five. “Wanna draw with me?”

🌟 Chapter 5: Stronger Than a Pen

Jonah didn’t forget the pen breaking—but he learned something more important.

He could feel big emotions… and calm them down.

He wasn’t a cry-baby. He was growing braver, smarter, and stronger.

🛏️ Chapter 6: Jonah’s Quiet Power

That night, Jonah whispered the Calm-Down Code again:

“Stop. Breathe. Think. Try.
I don’t let anger or sadness win.
I am calm, clever, and in control.”

🌱 Reflect Together

  • Can you remember the Calm-Down Code: Stop, Breathe, Think, Try?
  • What do you feel in your body when you're upset?
  • What helps you feel calm again?
  • How do you feel after calming down instead of staying upset?
