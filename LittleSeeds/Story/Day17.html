DAY 17

Zane and the Circle of Good Friends

FriendshipRespectTrustValuesChoosing the Right People

🌱 Reflect Together

Zane was excited to make friends at his new school. He noticed a group of kids who laughed loudly, played rough, and made fun of others when teachers weren’t looking.

“They’re cool,” Zane thought. “Maybe if I join them, I’ll be cool too.”

But something didn’t feel right in his belly. He watched them more carefully.

One day, Zane saw them push a quiet kid out of the lunch line. He felt his heart beat faster, like it was warning him.

Later at home, he told his mom, “They’re fun… but mean sometimes. I don’t like it.”

She nodded, “That’s your inner compass. It helps you choose friends who treat others well—and who treat you well too.”

The next day, Zane sat near a quiet table. One boy smiled and said, “Hi, I’m Amir. Want to draw with us?”

They laughed gently, shared markers, and helped each other fix mistakes. Nobody got teased. Nobody was left out.

It felt calm. It felt good. It felt… right.

That night, Zane asked, “Mom, how do I know if someone’s a good friend?”

She replied, “Good friends make you feel safe, seen, and strong. And being a good friend means you help others feel that too.”

Zane nodded. “So I don’t have to try to be cool?”

“Nope,” she smiled. “Just be kind, be real, and you’ll find your circle.”

Over time, Zane’s new group grew. More kind kids joined. They played fair, shared ideas, and looked out for each other.

Zane felt strong—not because he followed others, but because he followed what was right.

Before bed, Zane whispered,

“I choose friends who lift me high,
Who speak with truth and never lie.
I will be kind, honest, and true—
And choose the best in what I do.”
  • What makes someone a good friend?
  • How do you feel around people who are kind and respectful?
  • Have you ever felt uncomfortable with how someone treated others?
  • How can you be the kind of friend you’d want to have?
