Built to be Seattle’s most accountable contractor

KlyneDarius Construction Corporation is a residential construction company based in Seattle, Washington, formed around a simple premise: homeowners deserve one team that owns their project from first sketch to final inspection.

Our Story

A newly launched company, built on an old standard

KlyneDarius is a newly launched construction company — and we built it deliberately around the gaps that frustrate most homeowners during a remodel or new build: disconnected designers and builders, permit timelines nobody explains, and contractors who go quiet between draws.

We brought planning, design, permitting, and construction under a single roof so the people drafting your plans are the same people accountable for building them. That structure is slower to scale than subcontracting everything out — and it is exactly why we chose it.

We serve homeowners, property investors, and small business owners across Western Washington, from Seattle bungalow remodels to ground-up custom homes.
