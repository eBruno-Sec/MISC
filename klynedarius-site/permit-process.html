How a project moves from idea to inspection

Permitting is the part of construction homeowners understand least and worry about most. Here is the actual sequence we follow on a typical Seattle residential project, end to end.

Durations below are typical ranges based on standard Seattle/King County review cycles. Your project’s actual timeline depends on scope, jurisdiction workload, and review feedback.

01
Discovery

Initial Consultation

We walk the property (or review it remotely for early-stage projects), discuss goals, budget range, and constraints, and give you an honest read on feasibility before any drawings begin.

Typical Duration: 1–2 weeksYou Provide: Goals, budget range, property access
02
Design

Planning & Architectural Drafting

Our drafting team produces site plans and architectural drawings reflecting your goals and applicable zoning, setback, and lot coverage rules.

Typical Duration: 3–6 weeksDeliverable: Preliminary design set
03
Documentation

Construction Documents & Permit Drawings

Preliminary designs are converted into a permit-ready construction document set — structural notes, dimensions, and details that match what your project actually needs to be approved.

Typical Duration: 2–4 weeksDeliverable: Full permit set
04
Filing

Permit Submission

We submit your permit set to the applicable jurisdiction (Seattle Department of Construction & Inspections or the relevant King County office) and track the application on your behalf.

Typical Duration: 1 week to fileOwner Action: Permit fees due at submission
05
Review

Plan Review & Corrections

The jurisdiction reviews the submitted set and returns comments or correction requests. We respond directly so you are never the one interpreting city redlines.

Typical Duration: 4–12 weeks, jurisdiction-dependentOwner Action: None — we manage corrections
06
Approval

Permit Issuance

Once corrections are cleared, the permit is issued. We finalize the construction schedule and confirm the crew start date with you.

Typical Duration: 1–2 weeksDeliverable: Issued building permit
07
Build

Construction & Required Inspections

Construction proceeds in phases tied to mandatory city inspections (footing, framing, electrical, plumbing, and final). You receive updates at each milestone, not just at the start and end.

Typical Duration: Scope-dependentOwner Action: Milestone walkthroughs
08
Closeout

Final Inspection & Sign-Off

After the final city inspection passes, we walk the finished project with you, close out any punch list items, and hand over permit documentation for your records.

Deliverable: Final approval & project documentation
Questions Homeowners Ask

Permits, demystified

Do I need a permit for a kitchen or bathroom remodel?

Cosmetic-only updates (cabinets, paint, flooring) typically do not require a permit. Moving plumbing, electrical, or walls generally does. We confirm this during your initial consultation.

Who is responsible for permit fees?

Permit fees are paid by the property owner directly to the issuing jurisdiction. We provide the exact fee schedule before submission so there are no surprises.

What happens if the city requests corrections?

We handle the response directly with our drafting team. You are kept informed but are never responsible for interpreting technical correction notices yourself.

Can construction start before the permit is issued?

No. We do not begin permitted work without an issued permit in hand — this protects you from stop-work orders and re-inspection delays later.
