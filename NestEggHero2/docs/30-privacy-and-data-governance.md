# Privacy and Data Governance

- Classify all data.
- Minimize collection.
- Define purpose before collection.
- Set retention periods.
- Support deletion and export.
- Separate anonymous learning state from authenticated profiles.
- Obtain meaningful consent where required.
- Do not sell sensitive financial or child-related data.
- Treat child-directed experiences as a specialized compliance area requiring legal review.
- Keep third-party processors documented.
- Maintain incident-response and breach-notification procedures.
