# Verified 2026 Retirement Facts

**Verified:** 2026-07-16  
**Jurisdiction:** United States federal  
**Authority:** IRS

## IRA

- Contribution limit: $7,500.
- Age-50+ catch-up: $1,100.
- Total for an eligible person age 50 or older: $8,600, subject to compensation and other rules.

## 401(k), 403(b), governmental 457 and TSP

- Employee elective-deferral limit: $24,500.
- General age-50+ catch-up: $8,000.
- Higher age 60–63 catch-up: $11,250 where applicable.
- Defined-contribution annual-additions limit: $72,000, excluding catch-up contributions.
- Relevant compensation limit: $360,000.

## SEP

- Maximum contribution: generally the lesser of the applicable percentage limit or $72,000.
- Maximum compensation considered: $360,000.
- SEP plans generally do not permit employee elective salary deferrals.

## SIMPLE

- General salary-reduction limit: $17,000.
- General age-50+ catch-up: $4,000.
- Higher age 60–63 catch-up: $5,250.
- Certain applicable SIMPLE plans have specialized higher limits; do not present $18,100 as universal.
