## Verified 2026 IRA phaseouts

### Traditional IRA deduction when contributor is covered by a workplace plan

- Single or head of household: $81,000–$91,000
- Married filing jointly, contributing spouse covered: $129,000–$149,000
- Married filing jointly, contributor not covered but spouse covered: $242,000–$252,000
- Married filing separately and covered: $0–$10,000

### Roth IRA contribution phaseouts

- Single or head of household: $153,000–$168,000
- Married filing jointly: $242,000–$252,000
- Married filing separately when spouses lived together: $0–$10,000

### Saver's Credit income limits

- Married filing jointly: $80,500
- Head of household: $60,375
- Single or married filing separately: $40,250
