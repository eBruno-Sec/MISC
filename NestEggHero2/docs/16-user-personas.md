# User Personas

## Curious child

Needs short explanations, concrete metaphors, no data collection beyond necessity, and parent-safe flows.

## Parent or guardian

Needs trust, transparent assumptions, family examples, and privacy clarity.

## Young worker

Needs first-account guidance, mobile speed, and simple comparisons.

## Freelancer

Needs irregular-income scenarios and clear separation of personal and business concepts.

## Small employer

Needs contribution-role clarity, administrative complexity warnings, and official sources.

## Mid-career professional

Needs tradeoffs, tax-year specificity, and scenario comparison.

## Near-retiree

Needs larger type, reduced cognitive load, withdrawal sequencing education, and prominent uncertainty.

## Retiree

Needs clear distribution rules, beneficiary education, and scam-resistant design.
