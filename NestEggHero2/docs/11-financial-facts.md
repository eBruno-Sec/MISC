# Financial Facts Governance

**Last fact-check:** 2026-07-16  
**Scope:** United States federal rules unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Statute and regulations
2. IRS or SSA published guidance
3. Department of Labor or other federal-agency guidance
4. Official forms and instructions
5. Secondary educational sources only for explanation—not authority

## Publishing rules

Every changing fact must include its value, effective year, jurisdiction, authority status, primary source, retrieval date, reviewer, and next-review date. Never copy a prior year's number forward.
