# SEO Blog Engine

**Last fact-check:** 2026-07-16

- Use crawlable article content, stable canonicals, unique metadata, XML sitemaps, and descriptive internal links.
- Use Article or BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList structured data where applicable.
- Structured data can help search systems understand content but does not guarantee a rich result.
- Markup must match visible content and should be validated with Google's Rich Results Test.
