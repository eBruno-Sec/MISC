# Social Security — Verified 2026 Figures

**Verified:** 2026-07-16  
**Authority:** Social Security Administration

- 2026 COLA: 2.8%.
- 2026 OASDI contribution and benefit base: $184,500.
- Employee combined Social Security and Medicare withholding rate shown by SSA: 7.65%.
- Self-employed combined rate shown by SSA: 15.30%.
- The OASDI component is 6.2% up to the taxable maximum; Medicare rules differ.
