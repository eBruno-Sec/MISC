# UI/UX Principles

- One primary action per view.
- Preserve visible escape routes and back navigation.
- Use progressive disclosure for complex financial detail.
- Keep paragraphs short and scannable.
- Place definitions near unfamiliar terms.
- Pair charts with text summaries and accessible data tables.
- Show assumptions adjacent to projections.
- Use inline validation and recovery-focused errors.
- Keep forms resumable.
- Confirm destructive actions and support undo where practical.
- Display completion progress without pressure.
- Do not encode meaning by color alone.
- Keep touch targets at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels as the product standard.
