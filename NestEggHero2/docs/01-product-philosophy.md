# Product Philosophy

## Product promise

NestEggHero helps people understand financial concepts, test decisions, and preserve learning progress.

## Principles

- Teach before asking for conversion.
- Show consequences, tradeoffs, and uncertainty.
- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Keep advanced detail available without forcing it on beginners.
- Use examples without implying universal suitability.
- Make saved progress portable.
- Avoid account creation until it creates clear value.
- Offer a dignified path for children, adults, professionals, and retirees.

## Anti-goals

- No gamified shame.
- No guaranteed-return language.
- No manipulative countdowns.
- No fake scarcity.
- No obscured disclosures.
- No personalization based on protected traits.
- No investment ranking presented as neutral education.
