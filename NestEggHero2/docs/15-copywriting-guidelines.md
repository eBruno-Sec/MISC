# Copywriting Guidelines

## Voice

Clear, calm, encouraging, specific, and nonjudgmental.

## Prefer

- “Here is what changes.”
- “This estimate assumes…”
- “You can review this before continuing.”
- “Based on the values entered…”

## Avoid

- “Guaranteed”
- “Risk-free”
- “You must”
- “Everyone should”
- “Get rich”
- Fear-based retirement language
- Shame around debt or low savings

CTAs must describe the outcome: “Compare my options,” not “Click here.”
