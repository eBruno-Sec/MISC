🔍 The Biggest Mystery in Teyvat
Is Paimon Secretly an Archon?
Your floating companion, your "Emergency Food" — what if she's one of the most powerful beings in Teyvat?
⚠️ Official Status: Unconfirmed — But the Evidence Is Suspicious
MiHoYo has never explained Paimon's true identity. But here's the thing: every single Archon in Teyvat has a name pulled from the Ars Goetia — a 17th-century grimoire listing 72 demons of various ranks. Barbatos, Baal, Morax, Buer — all Archons, all Goetia demons. And Paimon? She's Goetia demon #9 — one of only 9 Kings of Hell, the highest rank in the entire 72. She commands 200 legions, rides a camel with a glorious crown, and grants all knowledge of arts and science. Sound familiar? That's your travel guide.
✅ Evidence She IS Divine
She outranks Barbatos in the Ars Goetia. Paimon is a Great King (#9). Barbatos is a Duke (#8). In the original text, Kings are above Dukes. Venti is literally lower-ranked than your snack companion.
One of only 9 Demon Kings. In the Ars Goetia, only 9 of the 72 demons hold the rank of King — the highest rank. Paimon is one of them, alongside Bael, Beleth, Purson, Asmoday, Vine, Balam, Zagan, and Belial. Bael (#1) is the Electro Archon. Asmoday (#32) is the Unknown God. Paimon is the only King whose Genshin identity remains unexplained.
Mona cannot read her fortune. When Mona tries to read Paimon's future, she gets nothing. The same thing happens with Archon Venti — divine energy blocks her. Paimon creates no elemental interference, yet still cannot be read.
Albedo's experiment reveals hidden power. Albedo calculates Paimon has enough latent elemental energy to shatter rock ten meters thick or reverse a waterfall. She looked confused — like she didn't know.
Her clothes have Archon symbols on them. The markings on Paimon's sleeves and legs exactly match symbols found at the base of the Statues of the Seven — symbols tied to divine authority.
An NPC says her string goes above the sky. One character describes Paimon as a balloon with a string extending upward — to somewhere above the sky. Above the sky in Teyvat means one place: Celestia.
She looks exactly like the Unknown God. White hair, floating crown, ethereal white clothes. The Unknown God (Asmoday) and Paimon share a very deliberate visual design. In 2025 teaser trailers, Asmoday is conspicuously absent while the other Three Shades appear.
❌ Evidence She Is Just Emergency Food
She has no Gnosis. Every Archon carries a Gnosis — the physical object linking them to Celestia's authority. Paimon has never shown one, and she shows none of the divine resonance that comes with it.
She can't control any element. Archons command entire elements at a sovereign level. Paimon uses zero elemental abilities throughout the entire game, including situations where any god would use them.
She's genuinely helpless in combat. Multiple Archons comment on how fragile she seems. Every other Archon — even ones pretending to be weak — carries themselves with underlying gravitas. Paimon screams and hides.
No nation. No ideal. Every Archon rules over a nation and embodies a core ideal — Freedom, Contracts, Eternity, Wisdom, Justice, War, Love. Paimon has none of these. She's attached to the Traveler, not any region of Teyvat.
The Unknown God was cold and ruthless. Asmoday coldly separated the Traveler twins without hesitation. Paimon is fiercely loyal, emotional, and protective. The personality gap is enormous — though a memory wipe could explain this.
💡 Top Community Theories
I
The Rebirth Theory
Paimon is a reborn or fragmented version of Asmoday — the Unknown God — with no memory of her original form. She transformed herself to observe the Traveler up close, either as a hidden watcher or as a form of protection for someone she recognized as powerful.
II
The Stripped Archon Theory
Paimon was a god before the Archon War — possibly Khaenri'ah's self-made deity — and was stripped of her authority and memory when the Heavenly Principles seized Teyvat. She survived as a powerless amnesiac until she found the Traveler.
III
The Watcher Theory
Paimon knows exactly who she is, and is deliberately guiding the Traveler toward a specific outcome — whether that means dismantling Celestia or restoring the old world. The "emergency food" persona is a performance. Every moment of vulnerability is calculated.

Before Everything
Unknown Past
The Primordial One Arrives — First Descender
An unknown entity descends from beyond the cosmos and imposes order on formless chaos. It creates the sun, moon, the laws of reality, and — importantly — the artificial sky of Teyvat. The world we know is built on top of whatever came before. This is the First Descender.
Ancient Era
🐉 The Seven Dragon Sovereigns Rule Teyvat
Before Archons and Celestia, seven elemental dragon-sovereigns presided over seven nations. Nibelung was the Dragon King. Apep ruled Sumeru's ancient rainforest. The Hydro Dragon commanded the Primordial Sea — the origin of all life. Azhdaha held Geo. Xiuhcoatl held Pyro. No thrones. No divine mandates. Just raw elemental power.
Ancient Era
🌙 The Three Moon Sisters & The Seelie
Nibelung creates three moon goddesses — Aria, Sonnet, and Canon — to watch over Teyvat. The Seelie, luminous beings of light, guide early humanity. It's an era of abundance and peace. These will all be destroyed in what comes next.
The Fall — Second Who Came (~6,000+ Years Ago)
Ancient
⚔️ Nibelung's War — The Dragon Age Ends
A second cosmic power — the Heavenly Principles — descends on Teyvat. Nibelung uses forbidden knowledge from the Abyss to fight back, but fails and is killed. The Heavenly Principles steal the Sovereigns' elemental Authorities, drive Celestial Nails into the earth to contain the Abyss, and dismantle the old world order. The Three Moon Sisters fall. The Seelie civilization collapses. Everything resets.
~6,000 yrs ago
🏙️ Khaenri'ah Founded — A Nation With No God
Humans build a subterranean civilization that completely rejects divine authority. No Archon. No Celestia. Just human ingenuity and the forbidden art of Khemia — alchemy capable of creating life itself. They thrive independently for thousands of years. This independence will eventually trigger the apocalypse.
The Archon War (More Than 3,700 to 2,000 Years Ago)
>3,700 yrs ago
💥 The Archon War Begins
Celestia creates seven Gnoses from the remains of a third descender and opens seven divine thrones from the stolen Dragon Authorities. Gods across Teyvat erupt into war for these thrones. Many scholars believe the Heavenly Principles engineered this conflict specifically so gods would eliminate each other — a controlled culling disguised as divine selection.
~2,600 yrs ago
🌬️ Mondstadt — Decarabian Falls, Barbatos Rises
Decarabian (God of Storms) rules Old Mondstadt behind giant walls. Boreas wages a war of blizzards from outside. A nameless bard, a wind spirit, and a knight lead a rebellion against Decarabian and defeat him. Boreas forfeits his claim, knowing he cannot be a kind and loving god. The wind spirit ascends as Barbatos, the Anemo Archon we know as Venti.
Ancient
🌿 Sumeru's Three God-Kings — and King Deshret's Fall
Sumeru was once ruled jointly by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, King Deshret (the Red King), and the Goddess of Flowers. A golden age of knowledge. But King Deshret pursued forbidden knowledge from the Abyss and became corrupted. He died. The Goddess was lost. Rukkhadevata ruled alone, setting up Sumeru's 2,000-year obsession with wisdom above all else.
~2,000 yrs ago
👑 The Archon War Ends — The Seven Are Established
The final throne is claimed. Seven victors become the Archons: Barbatos (Anemo/Mondstadt), Morax/Zhongli (Geo/Liyue), Baal/Makoto (Electro/Inazuma), Rukkhadevata (Dendro/Sumeru), Egeria (Hydro/Fontaine), Xbalanque (Pyro/Natlan), and Belyi Tsar (Cryo/Snezhnaya). Defeated gods flee to the Dark Sea. The seven nations are formally established. A fragile peace begins.
The Long Peace (2,000 to 500 Years Ago)
~2,000 yrs ago
📜 Zhongli Builds Liyue Through Contracts
Morax shapes Liyue through an intricate web of contracts — agreements that bind even himself. He forms the Yakshas, five adepti tasked with purging the world's karmic debt. He rules for the longest uninterrupted reign of any Archon in Teyvat's history, watching every person he grows to care about eventually die.
~500 yrs ago
🐲 Neuvillette — The Hydro Sovereign Reborn as a Human
An ancient Enkanomiyan prophecy said the Hydro Dragon Sovereign would be reborn in human form. And it happened. Neuvillette is born without full memories or powers — his Hydro Authority is locked in Egeria's divine throne. Focalors invites him to serve as Chief Justice of Fontaine, hoping he can eventually recover what was taken from him.
💀 The Cataclysm — 500 Years Ago
500 yrs ago
💥 Khaenri'ah Is Destroyed — The World Changes Forever
Khaenri'ah's master alchemist Rhinedottir (called "Gold") creates the black dragon Durin and unleashes synthetic monsters upon Teyvat. Whether this triggered Celestia's wrath, or whether Celestia struck first for other reasons, remains a mystery. The Heavenly Principles descend. Khaenri'ah is annihilated. Pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns are cursed with immortality. Those of mixed blood are transformed into the hilichurls we see wandering the world today.
500 yrs ago
😢 Five Archons Fall in the Catastrophe
Raiden Makoto (Electro) dies fighting. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata (Dendro) exhausts all her power cleansing Irminsul from the knowledge pollution and dies. Egeria (Hydro, Fontaine) dies — her body becomes the Amrita Pool. Belyi Tsar (Cryo) dies. A Pyro Archon also perishes. Only Barbatos and Morax — the two oldest — survive intact.
500 yrs ago
🐉 Dvalin Poisoned Over Dragonspine
Dvalin battles and kills Durin above Dragonspine, but swallows the black dragon's corrupted blood in the process. He retreats to Decarabian's old ruined tower to heal. He will not wake for 500 years — and when he does, the Abyss Order will have already found him and twisted his mind against Mondstadt.
500 yrs ago
⭐ The Twin Travelers Arrive — Then Are Separated
Two travelers from beyond the stars descend on Teyvat and get caught in the Cataclysm. The Unknown God (Asmoday) appears. She captures one sibling — who eventually escapes, joins the Abyss Order, and becomes its leader. She seals the other sibling (our Traveler) into deep sleep. The Traveler will not wake for 500 years.
Post-Cataclysm — The New World (500 to ~0 Years Ago)
~500 yrs ago
🎭 The Fatui Are Founded
Pierro, a survivor from Khaenri'ah, meets the Cryo Archon Tsaritsa. Both are changed by what happened. They co-found the Fatui — officially the diplomatic corps of Snezhnaya, secretly a rebellion against Celestia. Venti once described the Tsaritsa as the kindest of all Archons. Whatever she once was, that person is gone now.
~500 yrs ago
⚡ Raiden Ei Takes the Throne as "Baal"
After her twin sister Makoto's death, Ei adopts the title of Baal and pursues Eternity — a desperate attempt to preserve Inazuma exactly as it is so nothing else she loves can die. She builds the Raiden Shogun puppet to rule in her place and seals herself inside the Plane of Euthymia, cutting herself off from the world she's trying to protect.
>100 yrs ago
🌑 Abyss Order Founded — The Traveler's Sibling Takes Over
Chlothar Alberich founds the Abyss Order from Khaenri'ahn survivors seeking revenge against Celestia and the Seven. The Traveler's sibling eventually joins — and becomes their leader. Dainsleif, the immortal Twilight Sword of Khaenri'ah, becomes their declared enemy.
Recent years
💎 The Tsaritsa Begins Collecting Gnoses
The Cryo Archon directs the eleven Fatui Harbingers to take the Gnoses from each Archon. Zhongli surrenders his willingly. La Signora takes Venti's by force. What the Tsaritsa plans to do with all of them is the central unanswered question of the entire story.
Present Day — The Traveler's Journey (All Chapters)
Chapter I
🌬️ Mondstadt + Liyue — Traveler Awakens, Rex Lapis "Dies"
The Traveler wakes up after 500 years, meets Paimon, and frees Dvalin from Abyss corruption, restoring his friendship with Barbatos. In Liyue, Zhongli stages his own death — surrendering his Gnosis to the Fatui to test whether Liyue can stand without a god watching over it. It can. He retires to live as a mortal funeral consultant named Zhongli.
Chapter II
⚡ Inazuma — Vision Hunt Decree Ends, Ei Faces Her Grief
Raiden Ei's Shogun puppet enforces the Vision Hunt Decree, collecting Visions and freezing Inazuma in time. The Traveler fights the puppet, enters the Plane of Euthymia, and challenges Ei directly. She is forced to confront 500 years of unresolved grief over Makoto and the Cataclysm. The Decree ends. For the first time in centuries, the real Ei re-engages with her nation and her people.
Chapter III
🌿 Sumeru — Rukkhadevata Is Erased from All Memory
Nahida has been imprisoned by the Akademiya for 500 years while they ran Sumeru in her name. The Traveler helps free her. To save Sumeru from the knowledge pollution still corrupting Irminsul (the world tree that stores all of Teyvat's memory), Nahida erases Greater Lord Rukkhadevata from Irminsul entirely. Every person in the world forgets Rukkhadevata ever existed. Only the Traveler and a few others remember.
Chapter IV
💧 Fontaine — Focalors Executes Herself, Neuvillette Reclaims His Power
Furina has spent 500 years pretending to be the Hydro Archon while secretly keeping her divinity completely separate. She executes herself in court to dissolve the ancient prophecy that Fontaine would sink beneath the waters. Her death returns the Hydro Authority to Neuvillette. He pardons all Fontainians of their original sin and steps into his role as Teyvat's true Hydro ruler. The most emotionally devastating arc in the game.
Chapter V
🔥 Natlan — The Abyss Reaches the Gates, Il Capitano Falls
Natlan — the nation of fire and war — faces the Abyss directly at its borders. Mavuika, the Pyro Archon, has spent 500 years preparing Ancient Name bearers for exactly this battle. The Abyss invades the Night Kingdom itself. Il Capitano sacrifices himself. The path to Snezhnaya and the final confrontation with the Tsaritsa grows closer with every step.

❓ Snezhnaya & The Truth of Celestia — Story Ongoing
The Tsaritsa holds multiple Gnoses and her true plan is still unknown. The sky is artificial. History has been repeatedly erased. The Unknown God enforces a system of control, not divine benevolence. What happens when the Traveler reaches Snezhnaya — and what role Paimon plays in the final act — is the question the entire story has been building toward since day one.
📖 The Lesser Key of Solomon
Ars Goetia & Genshin Characters
A 17th-century grimoire called the Lemegeton lists 72 demons King Solomon supposedly commanded. MiHoYo named every Archon, most ancient gods, Paimon, and even the Unknown God after these demons. In Chinese, Teyvat's gods are literally called "Solomon's 72 Demon Gods." This was a deliberate design choice confirmed by MiHoYo in a 2021 interview.

⭐ The Key Finding About Paimon
In the Ars Goetia, only 9 of the 72 demons hold the rank of King — the highest tier. Paimon the demon is one of them. Bael (#1) is the Electro Archon. Asmoday (#32) is the Unknown God. That makes Paimon — the tiny floating companion who eats everything — the only Goetia King whose Genshin identity remains completely unexplained. MiHoYo has never addressed this. The silence has lasted since the game launched in 2020.
