The world

In the Reach of Aldermere, the buried Wellspring that feeds the five elements has cracked. Mourne, the Stillard — an ancient being who believes a changeless world can never suffer — separates two siblings and sets out to freeze the land into silence. You play one sibling searching for the other, guided by a small floating light called Tainer.

Five elements, attuned at Wardstones

⬡ Loam

Earth — raise cover, break weak stone, open buried paths.

≈ Tide

Water — fill mechanisms, extinguish, float objects.

✷ Cinder

Fire — ignite, melt Rime walls, controlled updrafts.

ϟ Arc

Storm — power devices, conductive chains, stun.

❈ Rime

Ice — freeze water into platforms, barriers, slow.

What you can do in the slice

  1. Select the brother or sister
  2. Choose a permanent class (5 to preview)
  3. Watch the separation & fish out Tainer
  4. Explore a 3D world in third person
  5. Fight Stonekin — non-graphic dissolve
  6. Earn a Mimic Orb & transform
  7. Use monster abilities with separate health
  8. Eject manually and automatically
  9. Keep your exact health on return
  10. Infiltrate a camp in disguise
  11. Attune all five elements at Wardstones
  12. Strike four elemental mechanisms — freeze the lake, raise stone steps, flood the basin, power the gate
  13. Fight elemental Stonekin variants (resist & counter)
  14. Duel Hush, the Hollow Warden — telegraphed slams, weak-point windows, phase two
  15. Find, equip, compare, dismantle & upgrade class weapons (3 tiers)
  16. Pass the Windrider's Trial to glide
  17. Gather timber, stone & ore; craft tools, blocks, traps & a forge; smelt iron
  18. Build & dig — place walls, lay elemental traps, mine the world
  19. Descend the Sunken Barrow into a crystal cavern
  20. Chain elemental reactions; dash, double-jump & charge attacks
  21. Slay Vornrath, the Deepwyrm to unlock the Wellspring Transmute & Deepened mode
  22. Read the minimap & world map as fog of war lifts
  23. Save & reload progress (.json, migrating schema)
  24. Build your own glade in Emberkin Studio & instantly playtest it
  25. Tune accessibility & themes
Honest scope. This is a front-end vertical slice plus a full design package — not the shipped platform. The brief's multiplayer, containerized, moderated live-service with user-generated content is fully designed in the Design Codex and clearly marked as not-yet-built. Local saves here are a convenience backup only and are never authoritative — matching the brief's rule that client state never governs real progression, currency, or child data.
