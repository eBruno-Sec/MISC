How We Make It

Made Fresh,
Every Order

🥩

Select & Pound

Free-range chicken breast, tenderised to an even 1.5 cm for a consistent crunch in every single bite.

🌾

Double Coat

Flour, beaten egg, then hand-pressed Japanese panko. The double coat locks in the juices and builds that signature golden crunch.

🍳

Fry at Precision

Into 180 °C canola oil for exactly 5 minutes. A probe confirms 74 °C internal before every plate leaves the kitchen.

🍽️

Rest & Plate

Rested 90 seconds on a wire rack, sliced on the bias, and served straight to you. Never held under a lamp.
