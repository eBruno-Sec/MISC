The Process

Crafted in
Four Steps

01

Select & Pound

Free-range chicken breast, tenderized to an even 1.5cm thickness for consistent crunch in every bite.

02

Double Coat

Flour, beaten egg, then hand-pressed Japanese panko. The double coat locks in moisture and builds the signature shatter-crunch exterior.

03

Fry at Precision

Dropped into 180°C canola oil for exactly 5 minutes. No shortcuts. A probe confirms 74°C internal before every serve.

04

Rest & Serve

Rested 90 seconds on a wire rack, sliced on the bias, and plated immediately. Never held under a lamp.
